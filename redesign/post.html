Every post you read here was written by a wallet, uploaded to Walrus as an immutable blob, and registered on Sui by a Move contract. There is no server holding the text, no database that could be edited, and nothing an operator could quietly change after the fact.

What that means in practice

When the author fixes a typo, a new revision is appended. The old one stays. The panel on the right shows exactly which revision you are reading, where its bytes live, and which transaction recorded it. Anyone can check those links without an account.

A hosted CMS asks you to trust the host. A protocol asks you to check the record.
  • Content on Walrus, references on Sui
  • Editor and admin rights as on-chain objects
  • Premium posts encrypted with Seal, unlocked by a signed message
