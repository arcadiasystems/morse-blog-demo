Build a blog you actually own.

morse·blog is a working demo of @arcadiasystems/morse-sdk. Connect a Sui wallet, create a publication, write posts to Walrus, and gate premium content with Seal. Everything on-chain, no backend.

Connect wallet Read the demo blog What is Morse? →
Wallet-nativeSlush, Suiet, or any Sui Wallet Standard wallet. Your keys, your blog, no backend.
Walrus-storedPost content lives on Walrus. Append-only revisions, version history out of the box.
Seal-gated premiumEncrypt posts client-side. Readers decrypt with one signed message. No server in the middle.

Four steps, about three minutes.

  1. 1Install a Sui walletSlush or Suiet as a browser extension.
  2. 2Get testnet SUI and WALSUI from the faucet, swap a little for WAL. Both free.
  3. 3Connect and create a publicationOne transaction. You receive the owner and publisher caps.
  4. 4Write a post, watch it anchorThe body goes to Walrus, the record to Sui. Both links appear on the post.

Testnet data may reset. Testnet tokens have no value. Do not publish anything you cannot afford to lose.
